Part 01 — The basics

What a pipeline is

A live list of every sales opportunity that hasn’t closed yet, with the stage each one is in. It shows the future, not the past. And it is not a customer list: a list sits still, a pipeline moves.

Who
the customer and the decision-maker
How much
estimated value (₺)
Which stage
where it sits in the funnel
When
expected close date
Every opportunity in the pipeline answers these four questions. If one is blank, it isn’t an opportunity yet — it’s a name.

Part 02 — The funnel

TOFU · MOFU · BOFU: the three layers

Top, middle and bottom of the funnel. Each layer has a different question on the customer’s mind, so each one needs something different from the rep.

Widths are schematic, not Mozaik data.
TOFUAwareness

“What is Mozaik? Who are they? Do I need this?”

The rep: Get attention, spark interest, capture contact details.

★
At Mozaik: Instagram ads, trade fairs, a walk-in at the showroom.
MOFUConsideration

“Is it right for me? How is it different from the alternatives?”

The rep: Understand the need, scope the project, prepare the proposal.

★
At Mozaik: Showroom appointment, site measurement, moodboard, revised proposal.
BOFUDecision

“Do the price, delivery and terms work?”

The rep: Resolve the objection, pin down the terms, get the signature.

★
At Mozaik: Contract, deposit, production and installation dates.

Part 03 — Health

A good pipeline passes three tests

A big pipeline isn’t automatically a good one. Check these three before you trust the number.

Coverage

Big enough

Open pipeline ₺ ÷ remaining target ₺

The right multiple is 1 ÷ your win rate. Win 1 in 3 and you need 3× coverage.

★
At Mozaik: At a 25% win rate, ₺2M left to sell this quarter needs ₺8M in open pipeline — not ₺2M.
Velocity

Moving

Opportunities with no stage change in 30 days

Keep this number small. An opportunity that hasn’t moved in a month is rotting.

★
At Mozaik: If a hotel proposal has sat at “quote sent” since last month, call this week — or close it.
Hygiene

Honest

Close date in the past, still open

These records quietly inflate the forecast. Update the date with a reason, or close them.

★
At Mozaik: A Friday five-minute sweep of past-due dates keeps the Monday review honest.

The one thing to remember

A pipeline is a promise about the future — keep it honest.

Know the stage, the value, the decision-maker and the date for every opportunity. Move it or close it. And when one closes, log the real reason — that’s how next quarter gets easier.
